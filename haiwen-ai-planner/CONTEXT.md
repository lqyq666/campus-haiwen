# Domain Context

## Glossary

### Consultation Handoff

The concrete student facts, stated concerns, goals, contact intent, and conversation guidance transferred to a human advisor. It is not the assessment report and must remain useful without exposing internal scoring details.

### Assessment Score

An internal planning signal used by Haiwen to calculate paths and priorities. It is not a consultation CRM field and must not be presented as an admission probability.

### Student-Stated Context

The student's own description of their current concern and near-term goal. It is separate from the scored student profile and must not be inferred by the system.

### Manual CRM Field

A Feishu field owned by the consultation team, such as assigned advisor, sales status, or follow-up notes. Automated synchronization must preserve its current value.
