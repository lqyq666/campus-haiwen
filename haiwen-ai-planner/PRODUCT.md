# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Primary users are Chinese university students evaluating postgraduate recommendation and entrance-exam paths under incomplete, time-sensitive information. Internal users are planning experts and consultants who independently review anonymized profiles, calibrate deterministic rules, and prepare for student conversations without repeating the intake.

## Product Purpose

Haiwen is the domain and business source of truth for an AI-assisted postgraduate planning and acquisition system. It turns structured student profiles and traceable official school evidence into deterministic path, score, program-matching, roadmap, lead, and handoff facts. Success means students can understand and trust a relative planning assessment, request human review with explicit consent, and consultants can act from measurable evidence rather than unsupported promises.

## Brand Personality

Professional, measured, and transparent. The product should create calm confidence through specific evidence and plain language, while remaining candid about uncertainty, missing information, and provisional calibration.

## Anti-references

- Sales-first education funnels that lead with urgency, course purchase, or exaggerated outcome claims.
- Black-box AI interfaces that expose impressive-sounding conclusions without inputs, rules, evidence, or uncertainty.
- Admission-probability dashboards, “稳录/保录/必上岸” language, gamified lead scores, or consultant-only sales labels shown to students.
- Decorative admin dashboards that prioritize visual spectacle over long-form review accuracy and keyboard-efficient work.

## Design Principles

1. Show the evidence chain and uncertainty before asking for trust.
2. Keep student facts, expert judgment, and system prediction visibly separate.
3. Make the next decision or action obvious without hiding important caveats.
4. Use plain Chinese for people and stable structured codes for systems.
5. Protect blind review, consent, and private operational data by construction.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.1 AA contrast and keyboard operation for core workflows. Forms use explicit labels and actionable errors; status is never encoded by color alone. Motion is optional and respects reduced-motion preferences. Dense expert workflows remain usable on common laptop screens and adapt to mobile for read-only review.
