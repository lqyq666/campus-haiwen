# Real school dataset coverage v0.1

Dataset `real-school-data-v0.1` is an offline normalized seed for 2027 planning.
Every source document has `sourceTrust: OFFICIAL`; raw text is restricted to
the factual extract needed by its attached policy and evidence.

The committed data contains 5 universities, 5 departments, 11 programs, 18
policies, 12 official source documents, and 25 evidence records. The source
URLs are in `data/schools/real/sources.json`; hashes are generated by
`scripts/school-data/seed-real-dataset.ts` with `computeContentHash`.

## Freshness

- `CURRENT`: only 2027 pre-recommendation notices for 东南大学、南京大学、南京理工大学.
- `LATEST_OFFICIAL_HISTORICAL`: 2026 catalog/policy facts; they are context and
  never become a 2027 current fact.
- 南京邮电大学 has a 2027 catalog preview but no asserted program-level current
  recommendation policy. 杭州电子科技大学 has no verified 2027 policy in this seed.

## Missing facts

This seed does not assert 2027 planned enrollment, examination subjects,
score lines, English thresholds, department deadlines, or admission
probabilities without an explicit attached official source. It is regional and
computer-discipline scoped, not a nationwide dataset.
