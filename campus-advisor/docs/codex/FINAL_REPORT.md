# Campus Advisor v0.2 final report

Campus now acts only as the anonymous HAIWEN acquisition channel. It collects a backward-compatible structured profile through bounded configuration-driven follow-ups, calls the centralized Haiwen client, and renders Haiwen-owned scores, path, Programs, evidence and 30/60/90 actions without recomputing business facts.

The student report shows score bands and explanations, the top three risks, relative Program confidence, official publisher/year/freshness/source links, specific roadmap success signals and the professional-review CTA. Consent remains unchecked by default. Contact details are forwarded only to Haiwen, masked in logs and excluded from Campus conversation history. Browser events are allowlisted, PII-screened and sent with idempotent IDs; Lead/event POSTs with stable IDs receive one safe transport/5xx retry.

Verification: 15 Python tests, Python compile, JavaScript syntax, real local cross-repository smoke and full browser interaction passed. Campus user-owned untracked `.codex-spreadsheet-ref/`, `.codex-spreadsheet/`, `output/`, `outputs/` and `tmp/` directories were not cleaned, overwritten or staged.

Remaining operational limits are durable event queuing, real-expert calibration, broader Haiwen school coverage and live Feishu credentials. These do not change Campus's completed v0.2 channel boundary.
